Stratum Pool Pinger

Finding the closest mining pool node is critical for reducing stale shares and maximizing your hashrate. This tool benchmarks the network proximity from your specific network to global SHA256 mining pools.
How it works: We measure the round-trip time (RTT) from your browser to each Stratum node. Links with latency under 9ms are typically flagged as invalid/unreachable (unless you are on a local network). Click any row to copy the Stratum URL.

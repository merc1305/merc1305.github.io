Smart Stratum Proxies

Automate your failover and eliminate manual endpoint management.

Managing a mining farm manually is a recipe for downtime. When a pool endpoint goes offline or experiences a sudden latency spike, your miners might spend hours submitting stale shares or sitting idle before you notice.

What is a Smart Proxy?

A Smart Stratum Proxy acts as a localized "traffic controller" for your mining hardware. Instead of pointing your miners directly to various global endpoints, you point them to the proxy running on your local network.

Key Benefits

  • Automatic Failover: The proxy constantly monitors multiple pool backends. If the primary connection slows down, it switches to a secondary link instantly, without restarting the miner.
  • Connection Aggregation: Hundreds of miners can be seen by the pool as a single high-hashrate connection, reducing network overhead.
  • Latency Optimization: The proxy can dynamically select the lowest-latency path based on real-time network conditions.
